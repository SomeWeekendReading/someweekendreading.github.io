This has been a pretty good year for the Weekend Hydrangea Team.  Let's check in on them.


## _Florilegium Hydrange&aelig;_

This has been a pretty good year for the hydrangeas.  Not as good as 2024 (see
[midsummer]({{ site.baseurl }}/2024-jul-04/#in-hydrangeae-veritas) and
[later summer]({{ site.baseurl }}/hydrangeae-2024/)).  But still, pretty good.

<a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/2026-09-30-hydrangeae-2026-hostas.jpg"><img src="{{ site.baseurelll }}/images/2026-09-30-hydrangeae-2026-hostas-thumb.jpg" width="400" height="300" alt="The hostas provided stiff competition for the title of most elegant flora this year" title="The hostas provided stiff competition for the title of most elegant flora this year" style="float: right; margin: 3px 3px 3px 3px; border: 1px solid #000000;"></a>

First, it must be mentioned that the Weekend Host of Hostas put in a pretty good showing
this year.  They are shown here, in front of and throughout the Weekend Editrix's garden,
with their delicate purple flowers poking up on very long thin shoots, somewhere between
1.0 - 1.5 meters above the ground.

I have a theory that this stem evolved to discourage squirrels and rabbits from eating the
flowers, which they apparently find quite tasty.  In years past, I have seen both species
nibbling hungrily on them, by going into the interior of the plant, pushing the stem down,
and walking out upon it to get the goodies.  But not this year?  I can't say exactly why,
but suspect that the litter of coyote pups and enthusiastic hunting by their parents may
have exerted some evolutionary pressure on the Squirrel Squad and the Lapin Cabal.

<a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/2026-09-30-hydrangeae-2026-hydrangeas-midsummer.jpg"><img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/2026-09-30-hydrangeae-2026-hydrangeas-midsummer-thumb.jpg" width="400" height="300" alt="Weekend Team Hydrangea at midsummer, with mascot at right" title="Weekend Team Hydrangea at midsummer, with mascot at right" style="float: right; margin: 3px 3px 3px 3px; border: 1px solid #000000;"></a>
<a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/2026-09-30-hydrangeae-2026-hydrangeas-early-autumn.jpg"><img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/2026-09-30-hydrangeae-2026-hydrangeas-early-autumn-thumb.jpg" width="400" height="300" alt="Weekend Team Hydrangea mascot, showing early autumn colors" title="Weekend Team Hydrangea mascot, showing early autumn colors" style="float: right; margin: 3px 3px 3px 3px; border: 1px solid #000000;"></a>
However, the gold medeal in the Ch&acirc;teau Weekend Flora Olympics must again go to Team
Hydrangea.

Here they are shown in 2 views:
- At the top, the team is in mid-summer splendor in their blue uniforms.  The team mascot,
  on the right, is in whites, but also in full flower.
- At the bottom, most of the blues are gone now, just one remaining visible in the
  background.  However, we see that the team mascot is now resplendent in the post-season fall
  red uniform.

The blues on the left and the red on the right invite political interpretation, but the
fact that the political red in the US is ugly while the red hydrangea is beautiful makes
that comparison somewhat inept.
