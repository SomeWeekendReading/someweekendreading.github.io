Doomscrolling is nowadays a way of life.  Sometimes life happens on trains, especially in Germany.  


## DOOM Runs on What?!  

Incorrigible readers of this Crummy Little Blog That Nobody Reads are, no doubt regrettably, aware of
our occasional pieces on the first-person shooter computer game DOOM running on hardware
that seems&hellip; inadvisable.  It's been made to run on pregnancy tests, in PDF 
files <sup id="fn1a">[[1]](#fn1)</sup>, in the statistical analysis language
[R](https://www.r-project.org/) <sup id="fn2a">[[2]](#fn2)</sup>, and to self-distribute
for installation by the internet Domain Name System. <sup id="fn3a">[[3]](#fn3)</sup>  

<a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/2026-08-13-doomscrolling-by-train-transrapid-1.jpg"><img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/2026-08-13-doomscrolling-by-train-transrapid-1-thumb.jpg" width="400" height="531" alt="DOOM running on the cockpit monitor of a TransRapid train in Germany" title="DOOM running on the cockpit monitor of a TransRapid train in Germany" style="float: right; margin: 3px 3px 3px 3px; border: 1px solid #000000;"></a>
Via writer [Charlie Stross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Stross) comes news from the
[FV Transrapid service in Germany](https://mastodon.social/@fv_transrapid/116388019166857212)
that, against all reason, they have gotten DOOM running on the cockpit monitor of their
trains, as shown here.  

The safety implications mean this is is on a whole new plane of inadvisability.  I mean,
it's hilarious as a joke, of course.  Just&hellip; _adjacent_ to some really bad ideas around
monkeying with the software of in-service trains.  

One hopes that sensible German train engineers know better than to do this on an
in-service train, especially while in motion.  I can't quite tell, but the view outside in
the photo does not _quite_ reassure me in that regard.  

[As Stross put it:](https://wandering.shop/@cstross/117059304741889123)  

> "Suddenly I have a feeling I know the real cause of the Transrapid 08 train crash at
> Lathen in 2006."  


## The Weekend Conclusion  

We cannot escape DOOM, as it is apparently found everywhere.  

I just hope we can &mdash; _somehow_ &mdash; escape the other sort of fascist doom, also
now found everywhere.
