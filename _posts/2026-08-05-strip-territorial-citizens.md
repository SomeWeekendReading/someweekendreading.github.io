Republicans want to deport all sorts of people from America, usually for racist reasons.
Now they want to 'deport'&hellip; Americans?!  


## A Bill to Do What?!  

Incorrigible readers of this Crummy Little Blog That Nobody Reads will recall we've
repeatedly screamed in frustration at the naked racism of Republican attempts to deport
people.  For example, the proposal to deport Native Americans <sup id="fn1a">[[1]](#fn1)</sup>
is _incredibly_ stupid, given that Native Americans have a _superior_ claim to a North
American birthright than anyone else!  

But one should never assume one has found the bottom of right-wing stupidity!  

<img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/2026-08-05-strip-territorial-citizens-congress-2.jpg" width="400" height="310" alt="Rep H Morgan Griffith (R-VA 9)" title="Rep H Morgan Griffith (R-VA 9)" style="float: right; margin: 3px 3px 3px 3px; border: 1px solid #000000;">
<img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/2026-08-05-strip-territorial-citizens-congress-1.jpg" width="400" height="284" alt="Rep Griffith @ Congress.gov: HR 9724, ending birthright citizenship in US territories" title="Rep Griffith @ Congress.gov: HR 9724, ending birthright citizenship in US territories" style="float: right; margin: 3px 3px 3px 3px; border: 1px solid #000000;">
For example, this genius: Rep H Morgan Griffith (R-VA 9).  He has introduced a bill into
the House, HR 9724 ("End Birthright Citizenship for Territories Act") <sup id="fn2a">[[2]](#fn2)</sup>,
which would drastically _change_ birthright citizenship for Americans born in these US
territories:  
- Puerto Rico,  
- the US Virgin Islands,  
- Guam,  
- the Northern Mariana Islands.  

And by "change", we mean _eliminate_ US citizenship for anyone born there after 2027-Jan-01!  

Children born in those US territories would become, more or less, stateless.  One presumes
they would not be exempt from US taxation, or US laws, or&hellip; anything else, really.
Just not US citizens, and not citizens of anywhere else in particular.  Probably unable to
travel at all, lacking any passports.  

Go on, read the bill.  It's only about a page long.  

Need I point out that the residents of these territories would be unlikely to be
considered sufficiently "white" by the standards of the white supremacists currently running the
Republican party?  

It just keeps going on: they want to restrict voting so much, and make it so hard, that
only their white conservative rural voters can do it.  

<img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/2026-08-05-strip-territorial-citizens-dd-1.jpg" width="400" height="223" alt="Democracy Docket: Republican National Committee proposes that Nebraska disenfranchise legitimate overseas voters" title="Democracy Docket: Republican National Committee proposes that Nebraska disenfranchise legitimate overseas voters" style="float: right; margin: 3px 3px 3px 3px; border: 1px solid #000000;">
For example, the sheer venom for everything outside the US makes Republicans even want to
disenfranchise American citizens who live abroad.  _Democracy Docket_
reports <sup id="fn3a">[[3]](#fn3)</sup> that the Republican National Committee itself is
trying to get the state of Nebraska to do exactly that: nullify the votes of Americans who
live overseas, but vote through former residence in Nebraska.  It's just an _insane_ level
of hatred for anything with a whiff of the outside world that makes them want to discard
_legal votes of American citizens_ just because they happen to live abroad at election
time.  


## The Weekend Conclusion  

Now, it's true that in Congress any representative can file a bill on any subject, at any
time, for any reason.  And they do!  Most of those bills go nowhere, legislatively.  They
are purely a virtue signaling mechanism to exhibit the beliefs of the legislator before voters and
other politicians.  

So this will almost certainly _not_ become law.  

But&hellip; consider: _this_ venomous hatred of anything outside their narrow definition
of American is what they _want_ to signal.  They think this is _virtue._
