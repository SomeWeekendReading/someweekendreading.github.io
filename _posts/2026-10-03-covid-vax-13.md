I got 2 initial COVID-19 shots in March 2021, and 1 every 6 months thereafter.  So here we
are at number 13, in a process that is now routine.  

"Isn't 13 vaccinations too many?!" is the panicky question likely to be asked by people
who haven't thought it through.  No, 13 is _exactly_ the right number for a senior.  Every
6 months, like clockwork.  

This particular version is mNEXSPIKE, the Moderna version for seniors.  As
[we discussed this time last year](https://www.someweekendreading.blog/rush-covid-booster/#whats-mnexspike)

when I first got mNEXSPIKE, the sequence has been biohacked a bit more than the original
vaccine (which had 11 different types of modifications to increase potency).  It's even
more potent, for seniors with weaker immune systems, and thus is administered at a
slightly lower dose (50&mu;g for SPIKEVAX vs 10&mu;g for mNEXSPIKE).  It just has mRNA for
the N-terminus domain and the receptor binding domain.  It also demonstrated _superior_
efficacy in seniors, not just non-inferiority.  So it's a good deal for us elders!  

All that is explained at the previous link, with pointers to the primary literature.
